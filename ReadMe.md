# VeriGreen - AI for Sustainable Fashion
VeriGreen Energy is an end-to-end AI auditing system that accepts draft energy marketing campaigns (copy, landing page URLs, or social ads), extracts environmental claims, audits them against substantiated regulatory frameworks, and rewrites them into compliant, high-converting copy.

Define the 4 core audit dimensions:
1. Vagueness Flag: Detects undefined buzzwords ("cleanest energy in town", "pure green" without metric).
2. Offsetting Disqualification: Flags any claim asserting carbon neutrality achieved purely through purchased carbon credits.   
3. Temporal Realism: Verifies that future claims ("Net zero grid by 2030") specify milestones and independent monitoring.
4. Guarantee of Origin / Scope Verification: Flags whether renewable tariff marketing explicitly identifies Guarantees of Origin (GOs), Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), or actual grid mix.
